# 🐉 DragonCraft: Prodigy Realm

A touch-first STEM + English adventure for kids (Grade 3–5), made for iPad Safari. Explore 12 voxel islands, solve math, science and English puzzles to cast spells, tame 25 dragons and fill your Dragon Book.

**Play:** https://kalyanmuppala.github.io/dragon/ — type your hero name and tap PLAY NOW. No login; progress saves in the browser on that device. On iPad use Share → Add to Home Screen for full-screen play.

## Features
- **12 islands**, each with its own look, mini-goal (collect items or a multi-spell combo), guardian dragon and boss.
- **25 dragons** to tame. Equip up to **3** to follow you; level your lead dragon to 10 for more spell power.
- **Dragon Book**, **6 hero looks**, island map and treasure collection.
- **Questions:** visual math (arrays, balance scales, fractions, area), science, and English (synonyms, opposites, spelling, plurals, word types, grammar, rhymes). Wrong answers show a hint — no punishment.
- **iPad controls:** thumbstick, big GO quest button, tap-to-walk; zoom/scroll locked; Retina canvas.

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step. Deploys to GitHub Pages automatically on push to `main` (version stamp busts the cache).
